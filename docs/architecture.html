The economics are the architecture. A five-minute explainer with nine visuals, where the director draws six and photographs three, costs $0.03 in transcription and nothing else. The same video with every shot generated costs $0.39 — thirteen times more for a result the grounding gate cannot verify. Preferring to draw is not a cost optimisation bolted on afterwards; it is rungs ① and ② of the ladder.

A forgotten WHERE clause is caught by row-level security. A misconfigured policy is caught by the application's own scoping. Requiring both to be wrong at the same time is the entire value, and it costs one SET LOCAL per transaction.
